A FEW MORE THINGS

Good to know.

Does STPlayer include audiobooks?

STPlayer is a player. It does not host or distribute audiobook content. Listen to your own files or content from supported public sources. Availability and terms depend on the provider.

Can I listen in the car?

Yes. STPlayer supports Android Auto with history, favorites, sources and search, as well as Bluetooth playback and Android media controls.

What languages are supported?

The app offers English, Russian, German, French, Spanish, Dutch, Finnish, Portuguese, Swedish, Turkish, Chinese and Japanese.

How does STPlayer handle my data?

Your library, history and settings are stored on your device. Release builds use Firebase Crashlytics for diagnostics. Online sources receive the requests you make; optional sync uses encrypted snapshots through independent relays. Read the full Privacy Policy, available in 12 languages.

How do I update the app?

Download the latest APK from this website or GitHub Releases and install it over your existing version. Avoid uninstalling first if you want to keep your app data.
