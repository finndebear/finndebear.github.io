How do I update?

Install the latest APK over your current version. You do not need to uninstall the app first.

What about privacy?

No account and no ads. Your library, history and settings are stored on your device. Production builds send diagnostic crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics. Online sources receive your requests. See the Privacy Policy for full details, including optional synchronization.

Are audiobooks included?

STPlayer is a player, not an audiobook store. Bring your own files or listen through supported online sources. Content availability and terms depend on the source.
