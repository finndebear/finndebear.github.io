Features ↓

Speed, bookmarks and a sleep timer.

01

Speed & sound

Adjust speed from 0.5× to 3×, set pitch independently, skip silence and normalize volume. Speed is remembered for each book.

02

Chapters & bookmarks

Resume where you stopped, choose a chapter or track, and save titled bookmarks. Listening positions survive app restarts.

03

Sleep timer

Stop after a duration or at the end of a track, with a fade-out. Optional motion reset extends the active timer.

Files, streams and downloads.

Online catalogs

Search supported sources by title, author or narrator. Catalogs and available downloads depend on each source.

Audiobook guide ↗

Your own files

Choose a folder with Android’s file picker. Play MP3, M4A, M4B, FLAC and other supported formats without changing the original files.

Offline listening

Download available books before a trip. Follow progress in notifications, cancel individual downloads and resume interrupted transfers.

Android TV

Browse books and control playback with a TV remote or mouse.

Android Auto

Browse sources, favorites and history, search and control playback through Android Auto.

Optional encrypted sync shares history, favorites, positions and bookmarks. Audio files and folder permissions stay on each device. Update all paired devices for large-library sync.

Install in three steps

  1. Download the APKOpen the file on your Android phone or tablet.
  2. Install STPlayerIf prompted, allow your browser or file manager to install the app.
  3. Choose a bookOpen a local folder or a supported online catalog.
How do I update?

Install the latest APK over your current version. Do not uninstall first. Back up your library before updating; backups do not include audio files.

What about privacy?

Your library and settings stay on your device. Production builds use Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports. Online sources receive your requests. Optional sync uses encrypted snapshots. Privacy

Are audiobooks included?

No recordings are bundled. STPlayer is a player, not a bookstore. Bring your own files or use supported sources; availability and terms depend on the source.
