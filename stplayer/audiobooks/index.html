How to listen to audiobooks on Android

Use your own files or a supported online catalog. STPlayer is free and requires Android 8.0 or newer.

Get STPlayerHome ↗

From a book to offline playback

1. Choose a source

Open a local folder with Android’s file picker, or browse a supported catalog. Search options depend on the source.

2. Start listening

Choose a book and chapter. Adjust speed, add bookmarks or set a sleep timer. Your position is saved for the next session.

3. Save for a trip

If the source allows downloads, save the book before leaving. Interrupted downloads can resume. Test playback without a network before relying on it.

Where can I find free audiobooks?

LibriVox and Loyal Books are independent catalogs of free public-domain recordings accessible through supported sources in STPlayer.

LibriVox

Volunteer-read recordings with browsing by language, title and author. LibriVox recordings are public domain in the United States.

Catalog information and terms — LibriVox ↗

Loyal Books

Free public-domain audiobooks, including many English-language classics. Available titles and download options are controlled by the catalog.

Catalog information and terms — Loyal Books ↗

Public-domain status varies by country. Check the rights and terms that apply to you before downloading. STPlayer does not host, bundle or sell recordings.
