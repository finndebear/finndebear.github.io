STPlayer is a free audiobook player, not an audiobook store. It does not host, bundle or sell recordings. Books come from your files and independent sources; availability and usage rights depend on each source and your location.

FROM DISCOVERY TO OFFLINE PLAYBACK

How to listen to audiobooks online or offline

You can use STPlayer without an account or subscription. Android 8.0 or newer is required.

01 / FIND

Choose a source

Open a supported online catalog in the app and browse by title, author, genre or narrator when that source offers those choices. You can also open your own audiobook files.

02 / LISTEN

Stream a book

Start listening online. Move between chapters, set a sleep timer, adjust speed from 0.5× to 3.0× and resume where you stopped.

03 / SAVE

Take it offline

When a source provides a downloadable book, save it in STPlayer before a trip. The app can resume interrupted downloads; the source controls which recordings are available.

FREE, INDEPENDENT CATALOGS

Where can I find free audiobooks?

STPlayer includes access to third-party catalogs. Two examples are LibriVox and Loyal Books, which describe their collections as free and public domain. They remain independent of STPlayer.

LibriVox

Volunteer-read public-domain recordings, with browsing by language, title, author and more. Recordings are public domain in the US; check the status of a work in your country before downloading.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before you listen

Is STPlayer free?

Yes. The Android player has no account requirement, ads or subscription. A third-party source may have its own terms; check that source before listening or downloading.

Are free audiobooks included in the app?

No recordings are bundled. STPlayer opens supported catalogs, including sources with free public-domain books, and plays your own files. It is a player, not a content publisher.

Can I download audiobooks for offline listening?

Yes, when the chosen source provides a downloadable recording. STPlayer can save supported books for playback without an internet connection. It cannot make unavailable titles downloadable.

Can I play my own audiobook files?

Yes. Choose a folder using Android's document access. STPlayer supports common audio formats including MP3, M4A and M4B, and remembers your listening position.
