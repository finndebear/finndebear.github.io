Back up your library before updating.

This release reworks internal library storage. As a precaution, create a backup in Settings → Backup & restore → Back up before installing the update. Library backups contain saved library data, not audio files.

WHAT’S NEW

Release notes.

  • Added DetiOnline for browsing, searching, playing and downloading audiobooks and audio tales.
  • Enabled audio downloads from YouTube and Patefon.
  • Expanded АКниги24 support with chapter playback, more sorting options, ratings and additional review pages.
  • Improved playback resumption after an app restart, preserving your chapter, listening position, speed and pitch.
  • Fixed seeking to the wrong position and losing pending seeks when changing playback settings.
  • Improved Android Auto browsing, playback resumption and error recovery.
  • Fixed catalogue loading stalls during playback and improved RuTracker mirror loading.
  • Improved preservation of history, favourites, bookmarks and the selected book during upgrades, backup restoration and sync.
  • Improved interrupted download recovery, cancellation and progress reporting through completion.
  • Kept different downloaded editions separate to prevent mixing their files and listening progress.
  • Improved restoration of search results, source selections and scroll positions when returning to a screen.
  • Improved TV remote navigation, keeping the selected result stable as search results arrive.
  • Localised more player controls, notifications and settings labels.
More release details

Behavior changes

  • Download replacement prompts now name the existing download and clearly explain that its files will be removed.
  • New downloads preserve the original audio and embedded tags. STPlayer no longer adds book tags or cover art to the audio.
  • Catalogue opening and sorting now respect each source’s defaults while preserving valid saved selections.

BREAKING CHANGES

  • The Bibe online source has been removed.

Risks / What to test

  • Upgrade from 0.0.95, restore a backup and sync between devices; check history, favourites, bookmarks and listening positions.
  • Restart the app and resume playback; check chapter selection, seeking, speed, pitch and Android Auto controls.
  • Cancel and resume downloads, interrupt the connection, and confirm completed downloads play offline.
  • Revoke and restore folder access; check that saved data remains intact and recovery works.
  • Browse and search the updated sources during playback; check sorting, additional pages, reviews and mirror loading.
  • Check TV focus, touch navigation and translated controls.
